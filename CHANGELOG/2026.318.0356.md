# 2026.318.0356 — test(substrate): move the kindcluster body's tests with the body

## Summary

`opencharly/spec#304` unit 5, the coverage half of the cutover. The `kindcluster` body lives in this plugin's CUE module (moved by `opencharly/plugin-substrate#35`), and its **five validation tests** still compiled `spec`'s shipped schema. **The harness is the host's own path.** `composedBodySchema` composes exactly what the kind-value gate composes — the spec base (`schemacompose.ServedSchema`) unified with this plugin's served unit (`schemacompose.ServedText`, from `NewMeta().Describe`) — and looks the defs up in THAT value. A body that stops composing with the base fails here, not at a deployment.
